The overlooked quarter: Frontier People Groups

Frontier People Groups are unreached groups with virtually no followers of Jesus (0.1% Christian or less) and no known movement to Jesus. Pioneer workers have to start with non-believers.

One in four people alive today is in a Frontier group

Each square is 1% of the world's 8.24 billion people. Joshua Project: 4,832 Frontier groups (2.07B people), 7,163 unreached groups (3.64B people).

Where the people are versus where the resources go

Share of each that goes to or lives in unreached groups. Joshua Project; Gordon-Conwell. The video Reaching the Unreached puts it at about 1 worker for every 250,000 people who have never heard.
